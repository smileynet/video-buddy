PRIMARY · CONFIRMED

Implement a technique in my project

Use a guide with prerequisites, versions, exact observed commands/settings, adaptations and checkpoints. Open source evidence beside the step when stuck.

Success: complete the task and verify the result. A finished note does not establish a working result.

Choose an approach with confidence

Compare alternatives against my constraints; separate claims, demonstrations, disagreements and missing evidence.

Success: explain why the choice fits and what evidence would change it.

Learn it well enough to use it later

Work an example, attempt a related task and answer recall questions. Keep answers linked to evidence.

Success: delayed recall and application to a new problem; note volume is not learning.

Find a solution while I am working

Retrieve a command, setting or explanation by task and environment, then jump directly to the supporting moment.

Success: find and use the right answer without rewatching the video.

Help someone else perform the task

Share an audience-specific runbook with prerequisites, expected results and known gaps.

Success: another person completes it with less explanation.

Act on a discussion or conflicting advice

Keep decisions, rationale and unresolved disagreement clear. Attribute statements when that changes the next action.

Success: act without misattribution. Speaker clusters cannot establish identities, owners or agreement.

SHARED FOUNDATION

Versioned evidence store + common timeline

Source identity · verified media · transcript and word times · separate speaker turns · frames and OCR · visual observations · guide steps · provenance and coverage

Begin with JSON manifests and atomic writes in the existing workspace. Add a database only when measured concurrency or search needs justify it.

Three separate status checks: extraction coverage (complete / partial), evidence support (supported / inferred / unverified), and execution validation (not run / passed / failed, with environment and date). A complete extraction is not a tested procedure.

Acquire once, reuse deliberately

Source adapters resolve identity and capabilities. The runner reuses verified media rather than downloading separately for speech and frames. Archive records never substitute for checking files.

Separate speech tasks

Caption/ASR, alignment and speaker turns have independent provenance. Preserve originals and merge for display. Unknown and overlapping speech remain explicit.

Inspect meaning after sampling

Scenes and time buckets propose frames. Text detection/OCR recover writing. The agent opens images, links observations to the user's goal and requests targeted follow-up frames.

Publish a supported result

A guide step carries prerequisites, action, observed settings, expected result and evidence. Mark demonstrated, inferred and unverified. Validation checks structure and references; it cannot prove the procedure works.
